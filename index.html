Set the clinical context

Design tip Include enough information for a defensible initial decision. Do not reveal the complicating fact here.
02 / THE TURN

Introduce a meaningful complication

This information stays hidden until the student submits Commit.

Design tip The complication should warrant reconsideration, not merely add another fact to summarize.
